About ShinsIO

Last updated 17 October 2026

ShinsIO is a small, independent research brand in early development. We look into the tools, plans and costs behind AI content creation and write up what we find in plain, dated research.

Creator Uncovered

Creator Uncovered: The Reality Check is our first research line. Each package and pack is a PDF that explains what a set of tools or a workflow really involves: plans, credits, limits, licences and the extra services you may need.

It isn't a shortcut to success. It helps you understand what you're getting into before you spend more time or money.

How we research

We check prices and limits against each provider's own pages, terms, help docs and repositories, and we date every check. Each edition shows when it was researched and when it should be re-checked. The details are on our Research approach page.

Independence

Our research is written for the reader, not for the providers we cover. Providers don't review or approve what we publish. If a product ever includes sponsored content or affiliate links, we'll label it clearly on that product.
