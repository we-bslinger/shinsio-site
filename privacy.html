Info / Privacy

Privacy

Last updated 17 October 2026

This site is run by ShinsIO. In short, it doesn't ask for your personal information, and we don't run analytics or tracking.

What this site does today

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No forms, and no mailing list.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking scripts.
  • Our code sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. The one script on the home page only runs the price summary and the app chips.
  • Checkout isn't live, so no payment details are collected.

Who receives your requests

Hosting. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you load a page, GitHub's servers receive the request, including your IP address and browser details, and may log it to run and secure the service. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Fonts. Pages load the Inter, Poppins and Raleway fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). Your browser requests them directly from Google, which receives your IP address and browser details. See Google's privacy policy.

Everything else (pages, images, styles and script) is served from this site.

Links to other sites

Sites we link to have their own privacy practices. This page doesn't cover them.

If this changes

If we add analytics, a contact form, a mailing list or checkout (PayPal is the planned payment provider), we'll update this page to explain what's collected and why before those features go live.
