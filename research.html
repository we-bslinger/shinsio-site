Research approach

Last updated 17 October 2026

How we check the facts in Creator Uncovered research, and how to read what we publish.

Where facts come from

  • The provider's own pricing pages, terms, help pages, documentation, repositories and licence files.
  • Checks done logged out, so we see what a new visitor sees.
  • Every check is dated. Each edition is a snapshot of those dates.
  • Prices are recorded in the currency we verified them in. ShinsIO's own prices are in CAD.
  • When we convert a price, we use the Bank of Canada exchange rate and state the date.

Evidence labels

Key claims carry one of these labels:

Verified
Confirmed on the provider's own page, repository or licence file, on the date shown.
Observed
Seen in hands-on use. We haven't done hands-on testing unless a section says so.
Calculated
Our own arithmetic. The inputs are shown so you can check it.
Assumption
An input we chose, such as a number of videos per month, stated so you can change it.
Reported
A claim from a third party that we couldn't confirm with the provider.
Unknown
We couldn't confirm it. We say so rather than guess.

What we don't claim

We never claim testing we didn't do. Unless a section says otherwise, we describe what providers publish rather than results from our own use.

Links

Links are checked when an edition is published. Providers move pages, so a link may break later.

Re-checks

Each edition carries a research date and a re-verify-by date, about 30 days later. For example, the Local AI edition was checked on 17 October 2026, with a re-verify-by date of 16 November 2026. After that date, treat details as possibly out of date until the edition is refreshed.

Corrections

If you find an error, please email admin@shinsio.com. See Contact for what to include. We check it against the provider's source and fix confirmed errors.
