Reporting a correction

Corrections help everyone. Please include:

  • Product, for example "Local AI Pack".
  • Edition: the research date shown in the PDF.
  • Page or section where the detail appears.
  • What's wrong, and what you think it should say.
  • Source: a link to the provider's own page showing the current detail, if you have one.

What happens next

We check the correction against the provider's own source. If it's confirmed, we fix it and date the change. How we verify is explained on our Research approach page.
